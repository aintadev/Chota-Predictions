# Chota Predictions 

Chota Predictions is a humorous, interactive browser game where you type a wish or goal and see if the universe is on your side. With a variety of belief systems, Hinglish/Banglish language support, and hilarious randomized predictions, it guarantees fun and laughter!  

## Features

- **Belief Systems:**  
  - **Astrology 🔮** – Predictions based on cosmic alignment and star vibes.  
  - **Aukatism 💸** – Assumes you lack “aukat” (social status/wealth); mostly hilarious “you can’t” replies.  
  - **Chotist 👶** – Treats you like a playful kid; funny, philosophical, and sometimes impossible predictions.  
  - **Gambler 🎲** – High-risk, high-fun outcomes; chance-based predictions with a thrill.  
  - **Random 🤷** – Totally unpredictable, chaotic, and absurd responses.  
  - **Philosopher 🧘‍♂️** – Deep, wise, and often humorous life lessons for every attempt.  

- **Languages:** Switch between **Hinglish** and **Banglish**.  
- **Themes:** Toggle **Dark Mode** and **Light Mode**.  
- **Easter Eggs:** 15% chance of triggering absurd and funny predictions for extra chaos.  
- **Responsive Design:** Works on mobile and desktop.  
- **Animated Feedback:** Includes loading dots and smooth transitions for predictions.  

## How to Play

1. Type your wish in the input box.  
2. Select a belief system.  
3. Click **Predict** to see your prediction.  
4. Toggle language or theme anytime to customize your experience.  

## Tech Stack

- **HTML**: Structure of the game.  
- **CSS**: Gradient backgrounds, glassmorphism, dark/light mode, animations.  
- **JavaScript**: Handles prediction logic, randomization, language toggle, theme toggle, and Easter Eggs.
